The four pages

Everything an active investor needs — nothing they don't

Stockdash is built around four focused workspaces. Each one does one job well, and they share the same watchlist, portfolio, and AI context.

AI Research

A conversational analyst that pulls fundamentals, technicals, peers, news, options, and macro — then writes a Bull/Bear thesis with cited numbers.

  • 14 research tools chained per prompt
  • Sessions saved per account · resume any time
  • Market snapshot · Fear & Greed · trending

Dashboard

A live, cap-weighted market map of every ticker you follow. Filter by sector, hover to inspect, click straight into the chart.

  • US + Taiwan coverage, USD-normalised
  • Watchlist sidebar with live quotes
  • Six-index tape · S&P · NDX · DOW · RUT · VIX · 10Y

Portfolio

Add holdings once. Watch live P&L, position weights, sector breakdowns, and per-holding day moves recompute in real time.

  • Total value · unrealized P&L · best/worst
  • One-click full AI portfolio analysis
  • Auto-saved to your account — survives every reload

Journal

Log every idea, open and closed trade. Filter by status, track win rate, and run an AI recap that turns your history into lessons.

  • Idea · Open · Closed states
  • Total trades · wins · losses · win rate
  • AI recap of closed trades + macro context

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Stockdash really free?

Yes. The hosted version is free. Self-hosting only needs a free Groq or Anthropic API key for the AI features — your account works either way.

Where does the data come from?

Yahoo Finance for fundamentals, history, and most quotes. Finnhub as a real-time backup for live quotes on the stock page. No black-box feeds.

Do I need to connect a brokerage?

No. You enter holdings manually. Stockdash never touches your broker — your trades stay between you and them.

Does it cover non-US markets?

Yes. Taiwan tickers (TSMC, MediaTek, Hon Hai, ASE…) ship out of the box, with USD conversion on the market map. Adding more markets is a config change.

Is the AI giving investment advice?

No. The AI is a research assistant — it summarises public data and structures pro/con theses. Nothing it outputs is personalised advice. Always do your own diligence.
