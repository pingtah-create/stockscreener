Everything you get

The complete feature set

Stockdash bundles 25+ tools that normally cost $300+/month across Bloomberg Lite, TradingView Pro, Koyfin, and Seeking Alpha — into a single workspace.

01 AI & Intelligence

AI Research Assistant

A chat-native analyst with 14 registered tools: fundamentals, technicals, news, options, peer comps, macro, screening, and more. Powered by Llama 3.3 via Groq.

Bull vs Bear Debate

One-click deep-dive on any ticker. Gathers fundamentals, technicals, peers, news, analyst consensus, and earnings — then synthesises a structured pro/con thesis with key numbers.

AI Company Intel

Business model, macro drivers, competitive moat, and key competitors — drafted by AI for any of 193 tickers. Cached on disk for 7 days so re-opens are instant.

AI Swing Setups

Daily AI-generated swing trade ideas with entry, stop, target, and risk/reward ratio. Combined with an algorithmic scanner that pre-computes signals across the universe.

News & Events Timeline

AI labels every significant price move in the last 90 days with the nearby news that likely caused it — earnings, downgrades, product launches. Cached 6 hours.

Session History

Every chat is saved automatically. Pick up exactly where you left off — up to 30 sessions per user, scoped per-account so your research never leaks.

02 Markets & Discovery

Live Stock Map

Squarified treemap of the entire universe, market-cap weighted, coloured by daily change. Spot rotation, blowouts, and red days at a glance — auto-refresh every 60 seconds.

Top Movers

Real-time gainers and losers, refreshed every minute. Built from the same in-memory cache as the rest of the dashboard, so it never drifts from your watchlist.

Fear & Greed Gauge

A daily market-mood score derived from VIX, with a short AI-written narrative explaining what's driving it. Helps you check yourself before adding risk.

Trending Stocks

The top 10 names ranked by a velocity score — absolute price change × volume surge. Surfaces what the market is actually talking about today.

Cross-Market Coverage

150 US large- & mid-caps plus 43 Taiwan tickers (TSMC, MediaTek, Hon Hai, ASE…). Taiwan market caps auto-normalize to USD using a live FX rate.

Sector Heatmap

11 sectors at a glance — instantly see whether today's tape is led by tech, energy, financials, or a sudden defensive rotation.

03 Charting & Technicals

Pro Charts (TradingView)

TradingView Lightweight Charts with three synchronized panels: price, volume, and oscillator. Smooth zoom, crosshair, and auto-extend on drag.

11 Technical Indicators

SMA, EMA, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, VWAP, ATR, OBV, Williams %R, CCI — all computed server-side with a longer lookback for clean edges.

Customisable Periods

Tweak the period of RSI, MACD, Bollinger, SMA, EMA right from the chart — your settings persist across sessions.

Drawing Tools

Canvas-overlay trendlines, horizontal levels, and annotations sit on top of the chart without ever blocking interaction.

News & Insider Markers

Headlines and insider transactions plotted directly on the price panel — see whether news moved the tape, or insiders moved before the news.

Auto-Extend History

Drag past the visible range and Stockdash automatically loads more candles — no awkward "no data" walls.

04 Screening & Strategy

Server-Side Screener

Numeric and boolean filters across the entire universe — P/E, market cap, dividend yield, growth, momentum, and dozens more. Returns in milliseconds.

Six Strategy Scores

Every stock is scored 0–100 on Value, Growth, Momentum, Quality, Dividend, and Deep Value. Sort, filter, and combine to build your own factor screens.

Swing Trade Scanner

Pre-computed algorithmic setups across the universe, refreshed every 2 minutes. Each setup includes entry, stop, target, and R:R — ready to act on.

Real-Time Quotes

Batch live quotes via Finnhub with a Yahoo Finance fallback. Watchlists and portfolios stay accurate even during the wildest open-bell minutes.

05 Portfolio & Workflow

Portfolio Analytics

Live P&L, position weights, sector breakdown, and per-holding attribution. Add or remove holdings and everything recomputes instantly.

4-Layer Persistence

Supabase → server disk → signed cookie → localStorage. Even on a serverless cold start with an offline database, your portfolio loads.

Synced Watchlist

One watchlist that follows you across devices and tabs. Powered by the same Supabase row as your portfolio, with offline-first fallback.

Full Portfolio AI Analysis

One click runs every holding through the AI debate engine and aggregates the results into a portfolio-level brief: concentration risk, correlated bets, and what to watch.

Trade Journal

Log every entry, exit, thesis, and lesson learned. Your journal is searchable and stays scoped to your account.

Cloud Accounts

Supabase-backed accounts work across devices. Optional self-hosted mode with flat-file storage for local-only setups.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Stockdash really free?

Yes. The hosted version is free to use. You only need a Groq API key (also free from console.groq.com) if you're self-hosting. Premium AI features and higher rate limits will land in a paid tier later — your free account will keep working.

Do I need to connect my brokerage?

No. Stockdash never touches your broker. You enter holdings manually (CSV import is on the roadmap), and we compute analytics from live market data only. Your trades stay between you and your broker.

Where does the market data come from?

Yahoo Finance for fundamentals, history, and most quotes; Finnhub as a real-time backup for live quotes on the stock page. Both are publicly available data sources — no black-box feeds.

Is the AI giving me investment advice?

No. Stockdash AI is a research assistant — it summarises public data and structures pro/con theses. Nothing it outputs is personalised investment advice. Always do your own diligence.

What if Supabase goes down?

Your portfolio is stored in four redundant places (Supabase, server disk, signed cookie, localStorage). If one or two layers fail, you still see your data.

Can I self-host?

Absolutely. Stockdash is a Flask app — clone the repo, set your Groq API key, and python app.py. Deploy to Vercel, Fly, or anywhere Python runs.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. We ship 43 Taiwan tickers out of the box with auto FX conversion to USD on the market map. Adding more markets is a config change.
