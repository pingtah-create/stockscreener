About Stockdash

A research workbench for retail traders covering both US and Taiwan equity markets — built by an active retail investor who got tired of stitching together five different sites for one decision.

What it does

  • AI Research Chat — ask plain-English questions and get structured fundamentals, technicals, peer comparisons, and swing setups.
  • Dashboard — live market map (treemap), gainers/losers, watchlist, and algorithmic swing scans.
  • Stock Page — interactive charts with 10+ technical indicators, drawings, news markers, insider transactions, and AI-generated company intel.
  • Portfolio — track holdings with live prices, sector breakdown, and AI-driven analysis of the full book.
  • Journal — log trade ideas, entries, and exits with screenshots and notes so you can review what worked.
  • Dual-market — covers S&P 500 + NASDAQ 100 + popular retail names and the most-traded Taiwan stocks. Most tools force you to choose.

Data sources

  • Prices & fundamentals: Yahoo Finance via the yfinance Python library. Quotes are delayed 15 minutes for US listings.
  • News: Yahoo Finance + Finnhub headlines.
  • AI layer: Llama 3.3 70B via Groq's inference API.
  • FX: TWDUSD live rate from Yahoo for Taiwan market-cap normalisation.

No proprietary data, no guaranteed uptime, no SLA. This tool is provided as-is.

Who built this

Stockdash is built and maintained by a single developer who actively trades US and Taiwan equities. The goal was a tool I would actually use for my own research — not a "platform" that nudges you to trade more.
